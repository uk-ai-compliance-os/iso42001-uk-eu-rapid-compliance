## The 7 Diagnostic Questions (What You'll Receive)

Every proposal is built from your answers to these:

1. How many AI systems do you currently have in production or pilot?
2. Do any EU citizens, residents, or legal entities interact with or receive output from your AI? (This determines EU AI Act scope.)
3. What is your current ISO management system certification status? (27001, 9001, 14001, none?)
4. What is your target date for ISO 42001 certification or EU AI Act conformity?
5. Who is the internal sponsor for this initiative? (CEO, CTO, CRO, Compliance Lead, Founder)
6. Which EU AI Act risk category do you believe applies? (Prohibited, High-risk, Limited risk, Minimal risk — or "I don't know")
7. Have you conducted any formal AI risk assessment, bias audit, or data governance review in the last 12 months?

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I know this will work for my specific AI system?**  
A: The diagnostic is designed to fail-safe. If I cannot map your system to a clear compliance path with high confidence, I will tell you within the first email exchange and you pay nothing.

**Q: We already have ISO 27001. Does this duplicate work?**  
A: No. ISO 42001 is designed to integrate with your existing Management System. The Roadmap explicitly maps which 27001 controls can be reused and which 42001-specific gaps need filling.

**Q: What if we need changes after delivery?**  
A: All sprints include one round of async revision within 14 days of delivery. Additional revisions are available at a flat £500 per cycle.

**Q: Why chat-only?**  
A: Because compliance architecture is a thinking discipline, not a performance. Async work produces better documentation, eliminates scheduling friction, and allows your CTO to review outputs at 23:00 if that is when they have focus time.
