## The 7 Diagnostic Questions (What You'll Receive)

Every proposal is built from your answers to these:

1. How many AI systems do you currently have in production or pilot?
2. Do any EU citizens, residents, or legal entities interact with or receive output from your AI? (This determines EU AI Act scope.)
3. What is your current ISO management system certification status? (27001, 9001, 14001, none?)
4. What is your target date for ISO 42001 certification or EU AI Act conformity?
5. Who is the internal sponsor for this initiative? (CEO, CTO, CRO, Compliance Lead, Founder)
6. Which EU AI Act risk category do you believe applies? (Prohibited, High-risk, Limited risk, Minimal risk — or "I don't know")
7. Have you conducted any formal AI risk assessment, bias audit, or data governance review in the last 12 months?
