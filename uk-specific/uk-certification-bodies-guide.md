## What Is UKAS Accreditation?

UKAS (United Kingdom Accreditation Service) is the national accreditation body.  
**Rule:** If your certificate is not issued by a UKAS-accredited body, UK enterprise buyers and regulators may not recognise it.  
**Always verify:** [www.ukas.com/find-an-organisation](https://www.ukas.com/find-an-organisation)

## The Four Major UKAS-Accredited Bodies for ISO 42001

### 1. BSI (British Standards Institution)
- **Accreditation:** UKAS-accredited for ISO 42001
- **Strengths:** Wrote the standard. Highest brand recognition. Preferred by FTSE 100.
- **Weaknesses:** Premium pricing. Longer lead times. Less flexible on remote audits.
- **Typical Cost:** £15,000–£25,000 (SME, 1–3 AI systems)
- **Contact:** bsi.com/iso-42001-ai-management
- **Best For:** Companies where brand of certificate matters to enterprise clients.

### 2. LRQA (Lloyd's Register Quality Assurance)
- **Accreditation:** UKAS-accredited
- **Strengths:** Fastest lead times. Strong in fintech/insurtech due to Lloyd's heritage. Remote audit friendly.
- **Weaknesses:** Less brand recognition outside risk/compliance circles.
- **Typical Cost:** £12,000–£20,000
- **Contact:** lrqa.com/iso-42001
- **Best For:** Speed-sensitive sprints (August 2026 deadline).

### 3. SGS
- **Accreditation:** UKAS-accredited
- **Strengths:** Global network. Strong in healthtech/pharma. Multi-site audits efficient.
- **Weaknesses:** Standardised approach — less tailoring to AI-specific risks.
- **Typical Cost:** £14,000–£22,000
- **Contact:** sgs.com/iso-42001
- **Best For:** Healthtech with international sites or EU subsidiaries.

### 4. Bureau Veritas
- **Accreditation:** UKAS-accredited
- **Strengths:** Competitive pricing. Good for first-time ISO certifications (27001 → 42001 migration).
- **Weaknesses:** Smaller UK AI-specific audit pool.
- **Typical Cost:** £13,000–£21,000
- **Contact:** bureauveritas.co.uk/iso-42001
- **Best For:** Cost-conscious startups migrating from ISO 27001.

## Decision Matrix

| Your Situation | Recommended Body | Reason |
|----------------|------------------|--------|
| Enterprise legal/fintech needing brand prestige | **BSI** | Client recognition |
| August 2026 emergency sprint | **LRQA** | Speed |

## The Certification Process (What to Expect)

1. **Application:** Submit scope statement + AI system inventory.
2. **Quotation:** Body reviews complexity and proposes fee.
3. **Stage 1 Audit (Documentation):** 1–2 days remote. Reviews policies, risk register, evidence structure.
4. **Stage 2 Audit (Evidence):** 2–3 days on-site or remote. Interviews + evidence sampling.
5. **Certification Decision:** 2–4 weeks after Stage 2.
6. **Surveillance:** Annual audit to maintain certificate.
