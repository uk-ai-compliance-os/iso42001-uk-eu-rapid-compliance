## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship relating to **ISO 42001 implementation, EU AI Act compliance, and AI governance advisory services** (the "Purpose"). In doing so, each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. This Agreement protects both parties equally.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by either party ("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing, electronically, by demonstration, or through any other means, including but not limited to:

- Business plans, financial data, and strategic objectives
- AI system architectures, model cards, training data descriptions, and performance metrics
- Customer lists, user data, and commercial contracts
- Regulatory compliance status, audit reports, and certification history
- Technical specifications, source code (if disclosed), and security controls
- Any information marked or identified as confidential, or that ought reasonably to be considered confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

3.1 Use the Confidential Information **solely for the Purpose**;  
3.2 Protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information (and in no event less than reasonable care);  
3.3 Not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent;  
3.4 Limit access to the Confidential Information to those employees, contractors, and advisers who have a strict **need to know** and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

## 4. Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

4.1 Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;  
4.2 Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, without restriction;  
4.3 Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;  
4.4 Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is legally compelled to disclose any Confidential Information by court order, statute, or regulatory authority, it must:
- Notify the Disclosing Party immediately (where legally permitted);
- Cooperate with the Disclosing Party's efforts to seek a protective order;
- Disclose only the minimum information required.

## 6. Term

This Agreement remains in effect for **5 years** from the date of disclosure of any particular Confidential Information. Either party may terminate discussions at any time by written notice. The obligations of confidentiality survive termination for the duration of the Term.

## 7. Return of Information

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, or upon termination of discussions, the Receiving Party must:
- Return all tangible copies of Confidential Information; or
- Destroy all electronic copies and certify destruction in writing within 10 business days.

## 8. No Warranty or License

All Confidential Information is provided **"as is"**. Neither party makes any warranty regarding the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, transfer, or assignment of intellectual property rights.

## 9. Remedies

Each party acknowledges that unauthorised disclosure may cause **irreparable harm** for which monetary damages may be inadequate. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek **injunctive relief** in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of **England and Wales**.  
10.2 Disputes are subject to the **exclusive jurisdiction** of the courts of England and Wales.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement is **mutual** — it protects both parties' information equally.  
11.2 **No partnership.** Nothing creates a joint venture or partnership.  
11.3 **Severability.** If any provision is invalid, the remainder continues.  
11.4 **Entire agreement.** Supersedes all prior discussions.  
11.5 **Counterparts.** May be executed by email exchange.
