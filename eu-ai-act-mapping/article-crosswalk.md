# ISO 42001 → EU AI Act Crosswalk
## How ISO 42001 Controls Satisfy EU AI Act Obligations

| EU AI Act Article | Requirement | ISO 42001 Clause | Evidence Typically Produced |
|-------------------|-------------|------------------|----------------------------|
| Article 9 | Risk management system | 6.1, 6.2, 8.1, 8.2 | AI Risk Register, Risk Treatment Plan |
| Article 10 | Data and data governance | 8.3, 8.4 | Data Governance Procedure, Training Data Log |
| Article 11 | Technical documentation | 7.5, 8.1, 9.1 | Technical File, Model Cards, Validation Reports |
| Article 12 | Record-keeping | 7.5, 9.1 | Documented Information Control Procedure |
| Article 13 | Transparency to deployers | 8.5, 8.6 | Instructions for Use, System Documentation |
| Article 14 | Human oversight | 8.5, 8.6 | Human Oversight Procedure, HITL Logs |
| Article 15 | Accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity | 6.1, 8.1, 8.3 | Accuracy Metrics, Penetration Test Reports |
| Article 16 | Quality management system | 4–10 (entire standard) | AIMS Manual, Internal Audit Reports |
| Article 17 | Conformity assessment | 9.2, 9.3 | Audit Programme, Management Review Minutes |
| Article 52 | Transparency to users | 8.5, 8.6 | User Notification Templates, UI Disclosure Text |

### High-Risk System Specifics (Annex III)
For UK companies with high-risk AI:
- **Annex III, Section 1 (Biometrics):** Requires ISO 42001 + biometric-specific DPIA
- **Annex III, Section 2 (Critical Infrastructure):** Requires ISO 42001 + operational resilience testing
- **Annex III, Section 3 (Education/Recruitment):** Requires ISO 42001 + fairness audit evidence
- **Annex III, Section 4 (Access to Services):** Requires ISO 42001 + bias testing across protected characteristics
- **Annex III, Section 5 (Law Enforcement):** Requires ISO 42001 + judicial oversight documentation

### Certification Strategy
1. Implement ISO 42001 AIMS using this toolkit
2. Use the crosswalk above to compile EU AI Act Technical Documentation
3. Engage a Notified Body (for EU) or UK Approved Body (for UKCA) for conformity assessment
4. Maintain post-certification surveillance via ISO 42001 internal audits
