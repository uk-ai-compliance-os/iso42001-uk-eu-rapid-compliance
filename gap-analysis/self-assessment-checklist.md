# ISO 42001 / EU AI Act Self-Assessment Checklist
&gt; **For:** UK AI companies with EU exposure  
&gt; **Deadline:** August 2, 2026 high-risk enforcement  
&gt; **Scoring:** 50 questions. Score 40+ = certification-ready foundation. Below 30 = urgent gap.

---

## A. Leadership & Context (Clause 4 & 5 ISO 42001)

- [ ] Board has formally approved an AI governance policy.
- [ ] A named individual (AI Lead / CTO / CRO) owns AI system outcomes.
- [ ] AI objectives are documented and aligned to business strategy.
- [ ] Regulatory scope includes EU AI Act if any EU user interacts with the system.
- [ ] A budget line exists for compliance architecture (not just legal review).

## B. AI System Inventory (Clause 6 & EU AI Act Annex III)

- [ ] All production AI systems are inventoried with name, purpose, and owner.
- [ ] Each system is classified: Prohibited / High-risk / Limited risk / Minimal risk.
- [ ] High-risk systems are mapped to EU AI Act Annex III category (Section 1–8).
- [ ] A "human-in-the-loop" decision map exists for every high-risk system.
- [ ] Third-party AI (APIs, embedded models) is included in the inventory.
- [ ] Retired AI systems are tracked with sunsetting evidence.

## C. Risk Management (Clause 6.1 & Annex A.5 / EU AI Act Art. 9)

- [ ] A formal AI risk assessment exists for every high-risk system.
- [ ] Risk acceptance criteria are defined and board-approved.
- [ ] Bias testing covers protected characteristics: age, gender, ethnicity, disability.
- [ ] Model drift detection (PSI, CSI) is operational with alerting thresholds.
- [ ] Adversarial attack scenarios are documented and mitigated.
- [ ] A risk register is maintained with ISO 42001 clause mapping.
- [ ] Quarterly risk review meetings are scheduled and minuted.

## D. Data Governance (Clause 6.2 & Annex A.6 / EU AI Act Art. 10)

- [ ] Training data provenance logs exist for every model.
- [ ] Data quality metrics (completeness, accuracy, timeliness) are defined.
- [ ] A data retention schedule aligns with UK GDPR / DPA 2018.
- [ ] Synthetic data use is documented and justified where applicable.
- [ ] No client-confidential data is used for model training without DPIA approval.
- [ ] Data subject rights (access, erasure, portability) are tested for AI outputs.
- [ ] Cross-border data transfers have Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs).

## E. Human Oversight (Clause 6.4 & Annex A.7 / EU AI Act Art. 14)

- [ ] Every high-risk AI system has a designated human overseer.
- [ ] Overseers have authority to override AI outputs without penalty.
- [ ] Annual training records exist for all staff interacting with AI.
- [ ] Override decisions are logged with reason and timestamp.
- [ ] Oversight effectiveness is reviewed in internal audit.

## F. Transparency & Documentation (Clause 7.5 & Annex A.8 / EU AI Act Art. 52)

- [ ] Model cards exist for every production AI system.
- [ ] Customers are informed when AI contributes to decisions (Article 52).
- [ ] An Algorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA) is completed for high-risk systems.
- [ ] Technical documentation (Annex IV EU AI Act) is draft-complete.
- [ ] A public-facing transparency statement is published (if required).

## G. Certification Readiness (Clause 9 & 10)

- [ ] An internal audit schedule exists for ISO 42001.
- [ ] A UKAS-accredited certification body is selected (BSI / LRQA / SGS).
- [ ] Evidence repository structure is defined and populated.
- [ ] Management review minutes cover AI compliance.
- [ ] Corrective action process is tested with at least one closed loop.
