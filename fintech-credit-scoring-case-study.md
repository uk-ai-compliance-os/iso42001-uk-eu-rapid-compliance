# 📑 CASE STUDY: 7-Day ISO 42001 Implementation for UK Fintech

**Client Profile:** A Series A Fintech startup based in London, developing an AI-driven credit scoring engine for SME lending.
**The Problem:** An EU-based Tier-1 bank requested ISO 42001 certification and a Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) as part of their vendor due diligence. The startup had 10 days to provide evidence or lose a £150k/year contract.
**The Constraint:** The CTO had no time for "discovery calls" or "consultancy workshops."

---

## 🛠️ The Async Solution

Instead of a traditional 6-month engagement, the client used our **Rapid Implementation Toolkit**.

### Day 1: Automated Diagnostic
The CTO ran the `gap-analyzer.py` tool. It identified 14 critical gaps in Annex A controls, specifically around **A.8 (Data Quality)** and **A.10 (AI Impact Assessment)**.

### Day 2: Async Onboarding
The client emailed the diagnostic report. We replied with 5 targeted questions regarding their model training data and human-oversight protocols. No Zoom calls were required.

### Day 3-5: Evidence Architecture
We built a private GitHub repository for the client and delivered:
- **Tailored AI Policy**: Specific to UK FCA requirements and EU AI Act Annex III.
- **Pre-mapped Risk Register**: 12 specific credit-scoring risks with ISO 42001 control mappings.
- **Technical Documentation**: Article 11 compliant documentation for their scoring model.

### Day 6: FRIA & Human Oversight
Delivery of the **Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA)**, documenting how they mitigate bias in lending decisions.

### Day 7: Audit-Ready Handover
Final review of the evidence pack. The client presented this "Audit-Ready" repository to the bank’s compliance team.
