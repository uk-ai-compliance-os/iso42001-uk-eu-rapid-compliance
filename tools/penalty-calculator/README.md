# EU AI Act Penalty Calculator

**Free tool for UK AI companies to estimate EU AI Act fine exposure.**

## What It Does

Calculates your potential penalty under EU AI Act Article 99 based on:
- Annual global revenue
- AI sector (Fintech, Healthtech, SaaS, Legaltech, Insurtech)
- Number of AI systems in production
- EU customer/user exposure
- Company size

## Legal Basis

- **EU AI Act (2024/1689) Article 99**: Administrative fines
- **Fixed penalty**: €7,500,000 OR 1.5% of global annual turnover (whichever is higher)
- **Enforcement date**: August 2, 2026 (high-risk systems)
