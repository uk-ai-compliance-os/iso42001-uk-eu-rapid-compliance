## The Core Distinction

| Attribute | ISO 42001:2023 | EU AI Act (2024/1689) |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Type** | International management system standard | EU legal regulation |
| **Purpose** | Build an AI Management System (AIMS) | Ensure safe, lawful AI in the EU market |
| **Enforcement** | Voluntary certification (market-driven) | Legally binding (regulator-enforced) |
| **Penalty for non-compliance** | Loss of competitive advantage | Up to €35m or 7% global turnover |
| **Geographic scope** | Global | EU market + any system affecting EU persons |
| **UK applicability** | Full applicability | Applies if EU users/customers exist |

---

## Can ISO 42001 Certification Satisfy EU AI Act Requirements?

**Partially, but not completely.**

ISO 42001 provides the **management system framework** that supports EU AI Act compliance. However, the EU AI Act contains **specific legal obligations** that ISO 42001 does not explicitly mandate:

### What ISO 42001 Covers Well

| EU AI Act Requirement | ISO 42001 Mapping | Coverage Quality |
|-----------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Risk management system (Art. 9) | Clause 6.1 + Annex A.5 | ✅ Strong |
| Data governance (Art. 10) | Clause 6.2 + Annex A.6 | ✅ Strong |
| Technical documentation (Art. 11) | Clause 7.5 + Annex A.8 | ✅ Good |
| Record keeping (Art. 12) | Clause 7.5 + Clause 9.1 | ✅ Good |
| Quality management system (Art. 17) | Entire ISO 42001 standard | ✅ Strong |

### What ISO 42001 Does NOT Explicitly Cover

| EU AI Act Requirement | Gap | What You Must Add |
|-----------------------|-----|-------------------|
| **Conformity assessment & CE marking** (Art. 20, 43) | Not in ISO 42001 | Engage Notified Body or self-assess + declare |
| **Registration in EU database** (Art. 49) | Not in ISO 42001 | Register high-risk systems in EU database |
| **Fundamental rights impact assessment** (Art. 27) | Partial | Add explicit FRIA template to your AIMS |
| **Specific transparency obligations** (Art. 52) | General only | Add Article 52-specific notice wording |
| **Prohibited AI practices** (Art. 5) | Not addressed | Add explicit prohibition list to policy |
| **Deepfake disclosure** (Art. 50) | Not addressed | Add synthetic media disclosure protocol |

## The Efficient Dual-Compliance Path

**Do not build two separate programmes.** Build one AIMS under ISO 42001, then augment it with EU AI Act-specific modules.

### Phase 1: ISO 42001 Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
- AI system inventory
- Risk register with ISO 42001 clause mapping
- Governance policy
- Data governance protocols
- Human oversight procedures

### Phase 2: EU AI Act Augmentation (Weeks 5–6)
- Annex III risk classification overlay
- Conformity assessment procedure
- Fundamental rights impact assessment (FRIA) template
- EU database registration protocol
- Article 52 transparency notice templates
- CE marking procedure (if required)

### Phase 3: Integrated Audit (Weeks 7–8)
- Internal audit against ISO 42001
- Self-assessment against EU AI Act checklist
- Gap closure
- Certification body engagement (ISO 42001) + conformity declaration (EU AI Act)

## Bottom Line for UK Companies

If you have **zero EU exposure:** ISO 42001 alone is sufficient. It builds trust with UK customers and prepares you for future UK AI regulation.

If you have **any EU exposure:** You need ISO 42001 **plus** EU AI Act conformity. The good news: ISO 42001 does 70% of the work. The remaining 30% is legal procedure, not architecture.
