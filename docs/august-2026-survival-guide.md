# The 90-Day EU AI Act Compliance Sprint
## For UK AI Companies | May – August 2026

### Executive Summary
UK AI companies with EU exposure have 78 days to achieve a defensible compliance position for the August 2, 2026 EU AI Act high-risk enforcement date. This guide provides a week-by-week sprint plan.

### Sprint Overview

| Week | Dates | Focus | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | May 14–20 | Scope & Classification | EU AI Act scope determination, risk classification, gap analysis |
| 2 | May 21–27 | Policy Foundation | AI Policy, Governance Board charter, roles assigned |
| 3 | May 28–Jun 3 | Risk Architecture | Risk register, impact assessments, bias audits |
| 4 | Jun 4–10 | Technical Documentation | Model cards, data governance docs, technical file |
| 5 | Jun 11–17 | Human Oversight | HITL procedures, escalation paths, training records |
| 6 | Jun 18–24 | Quality Management | AIMS manual, internal audit plan, management review |
| 7 | Jun 25–Jul 1 | Conformity Assessment | Notified body engagement, documentation compilation |
| 8 | Jul 2–8 | Testing & Validation | End-to-end compliance test, incident response drill |
| 9 | Jul 9–15 | Remediation | Close gaps identified in testing |
| 10 | Jul 16–22 | Final Review | Management review, board approval, certification submission |
| 11 | Jul 23–29 | Buffer & Surveillance | Post-market monitoring setup, ongoing audit calendar |
| 12 | Jul 30–Aug 2 | Go-Live | Final conformance check, enforcement readiness |
