# ISO 42001:2023 Certification Roadmap for UK AI Companies
&gt; **Timeline:** 12 weeks from zero to audit-ready  
&gt; **Assumption:** You have existing ISO 27001 or basic GDPR programme.  
&gt; **Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (England & Wales)  
&gt; **Certification Body:** UKAS-accredited (BSI, LRQA, SGS, Bureau Veritas)

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

| Week | Activity | Deliverable | Owner |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | AI system inventory + risk classification | Master inventory spreadsheet | CTO |
| 1 | Board mandate for AIMS | Signed policy charter | CEO / Board |
| 2 | Gap analysis against ISO 42001 Clauses 4–10 | Gap report with scores | Compliance Lead |
| 2 | EU AI Act scope determination | Scope memo + risk tiering | Compliance Lead |

## Phase 2: Architecture (Weeks 3–5)

| Week | Activity | Deliverable | Owner |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|
| 3 | Draft AI governance policy (sector-specific) | `ai-policy-[sector].md` | Governance Lead |
| 3 | Define AI risk acceptance criteria | Risk appetite statement | CRO |
| 4 | Build risk register + control mapping | Risk register + crosswalk matrix | Compliance Lead |
| 4 | Data governance protocols | DPA amendments + retention schedule | DPO |
| 5 | Human oversight procedures | HITL protocol + training plan | HR / CTO |
| 5 | Model card template + transparency statement | Documentation templates | ML Lead |

## Phase 3: Evidence & Implementation (Weeks 6–8)

| Week | Activity | Deliverable | Owner |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|
| 6 | Populate evidence repository | Evidence folder structure | Compliance Lead |
| 6 | Internal audit plan + checklist | Audit programme | Internal Audit |
| 7 | Conduct pre-assessment internal audit | Internal audit report | Internal Audit |
| 7 | Corrective action closure | CAR log with evidence | Compliance Lead |
| 8 | Management review meeting | Review minutes + action log | CEO |
| 8 | Select certification body + book Stage 1 | Booking confirmation | Compliance Lead |

## Phase 4: Certification Sprint (Weeks 9–12)

| Week | Activity | Deliverable | Owner |
|------|----------|-------------|-------|
| 9 | Stage 1 audit (documentation review) | Stage 1 findings + response | Certification Body |
| 10 | Close Stage 1 non-conformities | Corrective evidence pack | Compliance Lead |
| 11 | Stage 2 audit (evidence + interviews) | Stage 2 report | Certification Body |
| 12 | Certification decision + surveillance plan | ISO 42001 certificate | Certification Body |
