## What This Toolkit Is

The UK-EU AI Compliance OS is an **open-source compliance architecture** for UK AI companies facing the **EU AI Act August 2, 2026 enforcement deadline** and seeking **ISO 42001:2023 certification**.

**What you get for free:**
- Sector-specific AI policy templates (Fintech, Healthtech, Legaltech, Insurtech, SaaS)
- Self-assessment checklists
- Risk register templates
- Regulatory crosswalks (EU AI Act → ISO 42001)
- UK-specific regulatory context (BSI, UKAS, ICO)
- Automation scripts

**What requires bespoke engagement:**
- ❌ Tailored gap scoring matrix (sector + risk tier + regulatory exposure weighted)
- ❌ Customised remediation roadmap (sequenced, resourced, time-bound)
- ❌ Board-ready policy documentation
- ❌ Certification body pre-assessment preparation
- ❌ Auditor response scripts and evidence repositories
