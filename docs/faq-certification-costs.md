## Does ISO 27001 Reduce the Cost?

**Yes.** If you hold ISO 27001:2022 with operational Annex A controls, you can inherit:

- Risk management framework (ISO 42001 Clause 6.1 maps to ISO 27001 Clause 6.1)
- Internal audit process (Clause 9.2)
- Management review rhythm (Clause 9.3)
- Corrective action methodology (Clause 10)

**Estimated saving:** 30–40% on implementation time.  
**What you still need:** AI-specific policy, risk register, model cards, bias audit evidence, and human oversight protocols.
