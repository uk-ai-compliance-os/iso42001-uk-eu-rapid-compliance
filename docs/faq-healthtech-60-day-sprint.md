## The Reality Check

Can you achieve ISO 42001 certification in 60 days? **No.**  
Can you achieve **audit-ready governance** that satisfies an NHS Trust and positions you for certification? **Yes.**

---

## The 60-Day Priority Stack

### Days 1–10: Emergency Foundation
| Priority | Action | Why First |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | AI system inventory + MHRA SaMD classification | Determines your entire compliance path |
| 2 | EU AI Act scope check (any EU patients/users?) | If yes, August 2026 is your hard deadline |
| 3 | Board mandate + named AI governance owner | Without this, nothing else is credible |
| 4 | Clinical Safety Officer appointment | Required for SaMD and NHS governance |

### Days 11–25: Documentation Sprint
| Priority | Action | Deliverable |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 5 | Clinical AI governance policy | `ai-policy-healthtech.md` adapted to your system |
| 6 | Risk register (15 pre-built risks) | Customised with your specific hazards |
| 7 | Data governance protocol | NHS DPA + UK GDPR + ICO alignment |
| 8 | Human oversight procedure | HITL protocol for clinical decisions |
| 9 | Model card + validation summary | For NHS Trust review |

### Days 26–45: Evidence & Validation
| Priority | Action | Deliverable |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 10 | Bias audit across protected characteristics | Demographic parity report |
| 11 | Algorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA) | Required for high-risk under EU AI Act |
| 12 | Clinical validation study update | Performance metrics stratified by group |
| 13 | Evidence repository structure | Organised folder system for audit |
| 14 | Internal pre-audit (self-run) | Gap list + corrective actions |

### Days 46–60: External Readiness
| Priority | Action | Deliverable |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 15 | Management review meeting | Minutes + board sign-off |
| 16 | Certification body selection | BSI vs LRQA vs SGS for healthtech |
| 17 | Stage 1 audit booking | Application pack submitted |
| 18 | NHS Trust governance presentation | Slide deck + Q&A prep |

## What You Can Skip (For Now)

| Item | Skip? | Reason |
|------|-------|--------|
| Full ISO 42001 certification | ✅ Skip for 60 days | Book Stage 1 for Day 60+. Focus on readiness. |
| EU AI Act CE marking | ⚠️ Defer if no EU users | If EU users exist, this moves to Days 11–25. |
| Comprehensive staff training | ⚠️ Minimum viable | Train only staff touching the AI system. |
| Third-party vendor audits | ✅ Skip for 60 days | Add contract clauses now; audit later. |

## The NHS Trust Conversation

NHS Trusts do not require ISO 42001 certificates. They require **demonstrated governance.** Show them:

1. Your inventory and risk classification
2. Your clinical governance policy
3. Your model card and validation metrics
4. Your human oversight protocol
5. Your evidence repository

If these five items are documented and board-approved, you pass NHS governance review.

---

## The MHRA Overlap

If your system is **Software as Medical Device (SaMD):**
- MHRA requires UKCA/CE marking pathway
- ISO 42001 is **complementary**, not a replacement
- Clinical risk management (ISO 14971) runs parallel to AI risk management (ISO 42001)

**Do not confuse the two.** MHRA cares about patient safety. ISO 42001 cares about AI governance. You need both.
